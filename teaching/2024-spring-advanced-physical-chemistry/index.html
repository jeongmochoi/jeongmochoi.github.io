Course Overview

Course Code
CH6000375
Course Description
This course introduces the basis of physical chemistry and helps students understand the background knowledge of state-of-the-art research.
Time and Classroom
Mon 1:30–4:00 pm, Room 409
Textbooks
  • Donald A. McQuarrie, Quantum Chemistry, 2nd Ed., University Science Books, 2007, ISBN 9781891389504
  • Ken A. Dill and Sarina Bromberg, Molecular Driving Forces, 2nd Ed., Garland Science, 2011, ISBN 9780815344308
  • Atkins et al., Atkins’ Physical Chemistry, 11th Ed., Oxford University Press, ISBN 9780191082559
Prerequisite
Undergraduate-level physical chemistry
Evaluation
Midterm essay 50%, final exam 50%
