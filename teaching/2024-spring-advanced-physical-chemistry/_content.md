## Course Overview

<dl class="course-meta-list">
  <dt>Course Code</dt>
  <dd>CH6000375</dd>

  <dt>Course Description</dt>
  <dd>This course introduces the basis of physical chemistry and helps students understand the background knowledge of state-of-the-art research.</dd>

  <dt>Time and Classroom</dt>
  <dd>Mon 1:30–4:00 pm, Room 409</dd>

  <dt>Textbooks</dt>
  <dd><ul>
      <li>Donald A. McQuarrie, <em>Quantum Chemistry</em>, 2nd Ed., University Science Books, 2007, ISBN 9781891389504</li>
      <li>Ken A. Dill and Sarina Bromberg, <em>Molecular Driving Forces</em>, 2nd Ed., Garland Science, 2011, ISBN 9780815344308</li>
      <li>Atkins et al., <em>Atkins’ Physical Chemistry</em>, 11th Ed., Oxford University Press, ISBN 9780191082559</li>
    </ul></dd>

  <dt>Prerequisite</dt>
  <dd>Undergraduate-level physical chemistry</dd>

  <dt>Evaluation</dt>
  <dd>Midterm essay 50%, final exam 50%</dd>
</dl>
