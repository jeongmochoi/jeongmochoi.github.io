## Course Overview

<dl class="course-meta-list">
  <dt>Course Code</dt>
  <dd>CH6001559</dd>

  <dt>Course Description</dt>
  <dd>This graduate course introduces recent research trends with a focus on polymer theory. The objective is to understand the theoretical models used to describe synthetic and biological polymers. The first half covers foundational concepts, while the second half focuses on student presentations and discussion of recent research.</dd>

  <dt>Time and Classroom</dt>
  <dd>Wed 1:30–4:30 pm, Room 409</dd>

  <dt>Evaluation</dt>

</dl>
