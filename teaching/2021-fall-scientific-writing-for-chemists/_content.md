## Course Overview

<dl class="course-meta-list">
  <dt>Course Code</dt>
  <dd>CH76141</dd>

  <dt>Course Description</dt>
  <dd>This course guides students through the process of writing and publishing an original research manuscript. The students are expected to develop writing skills and understand the general structure of scientific papers.</dd>
