I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Chemistry at Pusan National University. My research group studies the molecular principles of living systems using statistical mechanics, molecular modeling, and computational chemistry.

We are interested in the interactions and collective behaviors of biomolecules, the evolution and design of biomolecular systems, and biological applications of statistical mechanics. I also write and translate books, give public lectures, and communicate ideas in chemistry and the history of science to broader audiences.
